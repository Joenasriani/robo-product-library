## Section 1: Industrial Robot Categories

Articulated robots are the most common type with rotary joints (4-6 axes) for welding, painting, and assembly. FANUC, KUKA, ABB, and Yaskawa Motoman are leading manufacturers.

Collaborative robots (Cobots) work alongside humans using force/torque sensing (ISO/TS 15066). Major cobot brands: Universal Robots, FANUC CRX, ABB YuMi, KUKA iiwa, Doosan.

## Section 2: Integration Standards

OPC UA is the recommended protocol for Industry 4.0. EtherNet/IP is common in North America; PROFINET dominates European Siemens environments.

## Section 3: Safety Standards

ISO 10218-1/2 covers industrial robot safety. ISO/TS 15066 covers collaborative robots. Every robot installation requires a documented risk assessment per ISO 12100.
