RoboMarket Platform Overview
============================

RoboMarket is an AI-powered B2B marketplace that connects buyers and sellers of industrial
robots, collaborative robots (cobots), robotic components, and automation systems. The
platform serves manufacturers, system integrators, distributors, and research institutions
across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

Key Platform Features
---------------------

1. Intelligent Product Catalog
   The catalog contains over 50,000 SKUs spanning six main categories:
   - Industrial robots (6-axis, SCARA, delta, Cartesian)
   - Collaborative robots (cobots) rated for human-robot interaction
   - End-of-arm tooling (grippers, welding torches, vision systems)
   - Motion controllers and servo drives
   - Safety systems (light curtains, safety PLCs, force-torque sensors)
   - Spare parts and consumables

2. AI-Assisted Sourcing
   Buyers submit a specification sheet or natural-language description of their
   automation task. The platform's RAG-powered sourcing engine matches requirements to
   products, retrieves relevant technical datasheets, and generates a ranked shortlist
   with justification drawn directly from supplier documentation.

3. Supplier Verification
   All suppliers undergo a three-stage verification: legal entity check, product
   authenticity audit, and reference customer validation.

4. RFQ and Negotiation Workflow
   Qualified buyers can send Requests for Quotation (RFQs) directly through the platform.

5. Integration APIs
   RoboMarket exposes REST and GraphQL APIs for product catalog search, order management,
   and supplier rating aggregation.

Supported Robot Brands
-----------------------
FANUC, KUKA, ABB, Yaskawa, Universal Robots, AUBO, Doosan, Techman Robot, Franka Emika,
and over 200 additional regional and niche manufacturers.

Pricing Model
-------------
RoboMarket operates on a transaction-fee model. Suppliers pay a tiered listing fee and
a 1.5-3.5% transaction fee on completed orders.
