## Robot Safety Standards
ISO 10218-1 covers safety requirements for industrial robots (robot design).
ISO 10218-2 covers safety requirements for integration and installation.
ISO/TS 15066 defines collaborative robot safety modes: safety-rated monitored stop,
hand guiding, speed and separation monitoring, and power and force limiting.

## Hybrid Search Concepts
Hybrid search combines dense vector retrieval with sparse keyword (BM25) scoring.
BM25 excels at exact term matching; vector search captures semantic similarity.
Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) is a common score fusion strategy.

## Cross-Encoder Reranking
After initial retrieval, a cross-encoder model reads each (query, document) pair jointly
to score relevance. This is more accurate than bi-encoder similarity but slower.
Common models: ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2 (fast), ms-marco-MiniLM-L-12-v2 (accurate).
