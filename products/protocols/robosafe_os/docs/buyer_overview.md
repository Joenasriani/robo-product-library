# RoboSafeOS Buyer Overview

RoboSafeOS is a deployment safety protocol pack for facilities that plan to use service robots, delivery robots, cleaning robots, patrol robots, reception robots, or autonomous mobile robots.

## Who buys it

- Hotel operations teams
- Mall facility managers
- Hospital operations teams
- Warehouse automation teams
- Premium residential property managers
- Robotics integrators preparing a deployment proposal
- Robot vendors who need a structured handoff pack for GCC customers

## What it produces

RoboSafeOS organizes buyer inputs into a practical deployment package:

- facility summary
- zone list
- restricted area list
- route rules
- time-window rules
- human traffic notes
- human review plan
- incident log format
- facility routing demonstration
- buyer export manifest

## Commercial position

This is a protocol pack and customization service, not a robot controller. It is sold as an inquiry-led package because every facility, robot platform, and operational workflow is different.

## Why it matters

Robotics deployments fail when the buyer treats the robot as the full solution. The missing layer is usually the operating model: zones, restrictions, shift patterns, operator responsibility, incident records, escalation rules, and review procedures.

RoboSafeOS provides that missing deployment brain in a format that a buyer, vendor, and integrator can review together.
