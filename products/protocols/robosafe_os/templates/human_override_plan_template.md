## 2. Review triggers

Document the conditions that require staff review.

Recommended categories:

- visitor congestion
- restricted area proximity
- unclear route assignment
- missed task window
- visitor complaint
- sensor or localization uncertainty reported by integrator system
- incident record requiring follow-up

## 3. Escalation matrix

| Severity | Initial reviewer | Secondary reviewer | Record required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| low | Operations desk | Facility manager | yes |
| medium | Facility manager | Vendor/integrator contact | yes |
| high | Duty manager | Senior facility lead | yes |
| critical | Senior facility lead | Site authority or approved escalation contact | yes |

## 4. Incident review notes

Every reviewed event should be recorded using the incident log schema. Keep evidence notes factual, short, and tied to observable facility information.

## 5. Scope note

This template is a planning artifact. It does not authorize live robotics activity and does not replace vendor procedures, local authority requirements, or site-specific engineering review.
