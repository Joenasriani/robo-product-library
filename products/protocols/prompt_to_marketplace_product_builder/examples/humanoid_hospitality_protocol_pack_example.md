# Humanoid Hospitality Protocol Pack — Example

## Product idea

A protocol pack for humanoid robots operating as greeting and concierge assistants in GCC luxury hotel environments.

## Target buyer

Robotics integrators and hospitality innovation teams.

## Use case

Guide respectful guest greeting, handoff, and escalation behavior for a humanoid reception robot.

## Example deliverables

- `README.md`
- `manifest.yaml`
- `protocol.yaml`
- `state_machine.md`
- `limitations.md`
- `support_policy.md`
- `listing_metadata.json`

## Limitations that must remain visible

- Does not certify cultural, safety, or regulatory compliance.
- Does not include a robot controller.
- Must be adapted by an integrator to the actual robot platform.
- Must be reviewed by the hotel operations owner before deployment.
