# Product Pack Model

A marketplace-ready robotics or AI product pack must separate an idea from a deliverable.

## Required sections

- Product idea
- Target buyer
- Use case
- Deliverables
- Setup guide
- Compatibility assumptions
- Limitations
- Support policy
- Listing metadata

## Product truth test

A product is not ready for listing when:

- Deliverables are described but not present.
- Pricing is invented without a policy or source.
- Runtime claims are made without runtime files.
- Compatibility claims are made without assumptions.
- Safety or certification claims are made without proof.

This framework helps create complete product folders. It does not create verified deliverables from a rough idea.
