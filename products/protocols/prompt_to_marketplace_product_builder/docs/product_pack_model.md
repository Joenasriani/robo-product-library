# Product Pack Model

A marketplace-ready robotics or AI product pack must separate an idea from a deliverable.

## Required product sections

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Product idea | Short plain-language description of the product. |
| Target buyer | Who can use the product professionally. |
| Use case | What problem the product addresses. |
| Deliverables | The exact files the buyer receives. |
| Setup guide | How the buyer uses or implements the files. |
| Compatibility | Known platform, middleware, or workflow assumptions. |
| Limitations | What the product does not do. |
| Support policy | What help is included after purchase/inquiry. |
| Listing metadata | Marketplace-facing title, summary, category, and status. |

## Product truth test

A product is not ready for listing when:

- Deliverables are described but not present.
- Pricing is invented without a policy or source.
- Runtime claims are made without runtime files.
- Compatibility claims are made without assumptions.
- Safety or certification claims are made without proof.

## Correct use

This framework helps create complete product folders. It does not magically create verified deliverables from a rough idea.
