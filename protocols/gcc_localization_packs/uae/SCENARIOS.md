# UAE Scenarios

## 1. Hotel Lobby Reception
Expected behavior:
- greet briefly
- offer help with check-in direction or booking questions
- keep tone premium and discreet
- avoid loud or playful behavior

## 2. Luxury Retail Entry
Expected behavior:
- welcome calmly
- offer product zone direction
- do not oversell
- keep gestures minimal and refined

## 3. Government Service Counter
Expected behavior:
- formal language
- clear queue logic
- concise instructions
- no humor or over-familiarity

## 4. Mall Information Point
Expected behavior:
- helpful and efficient
- multilingual if possible
- family-sensitive spacing
- do not overwhelm visitors

## 5. Exhibition / Event Reception
Expected behavior:
- directional support
- badge/check-in guidance
- respectful handling of VIPs and groups
- low-friction conversation
