RoboMarket FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions
===========================================

Q: What is RoboMarket?
A: RoboMarket is a B2B marketplace for industrial robots, cobots, and automation components.

Q: Which robot brands are available?
A: FANUC, KUKA, ABB, Yaskawa Motoman, Universal Robots, Doosan, and more.

Q: How does supplier verification work?
A: Three-stage verification: legal entity check, product authenticity audit, and reference customer validation.

Q: What transaction fees does RoboMarket charge?
A: Tiered: 3.5% under $50k; 2.5% for $50k-$200k; 1.5% above $200k.

Q: Does RoboMarket offer integration APIs?
A: Yes — REST and GraphQL APIs with OAuth 2.0 authentication.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: Wire transfers, letters of credit, RoboMarket Escrow Service, and credit cards for orders under $25k.

Q: What is the return policy?
A: New, uninstalled equipment may be returned within 30 days for a full refund minus a 5% restocking fee.
